Bloom Climbing Shoes

Climbing shoes with printed textiles

Scroll

Climbing shoes with friction rubber underneath and flowers on top.

Most climbing shoes put utility first and leave little room for personality. Bloom bridges that gap, pairing precision-engineered rubber textures for friction with bold printed textiles.

It comes as a slip-on and a dual-velcro strap version, in moody dark florals and a vibrant light pink: technical gear as a canvas for individual expression, without giving anything up on the wall.

03Workflow

From one sketch to a collection

I took a single line sketch into Vizcom, an AI design tool for industrial designers. Its node-style workflow gave me precise control for fast iteration, complex material mapping and colorway testing, while keeping the construction technical.

04Anatomy

Technical where it counts

The print lives on the upper. Everything that touches the wall stays technical.
